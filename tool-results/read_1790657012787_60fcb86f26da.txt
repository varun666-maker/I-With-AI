    53→## Design language (established in landing.tsx — match this exactly)
    54→- Dark background: `bg-background` (= near-black oklch 0.07 with subtle violet tint).
    55→- Aurora blobs (use `<AuroraBackground>` as page wrapper).
    56→- Cards: `GlowCard` with `p-6` and `border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03]`.
    57→- Primary CTA buttons: `bg-gradient-to-r from-violet-500 to-fuchsia-500 text-white border-0`.
    58→- Accent color words: `gradient-text` class.
    59→- Badges: `<PulseBadge color="violet|emerald|amber|rose|sky|slate">`.
    60→- Section title block: `<SectionHeading eyebrow title description />`.
    61→- Use Framer Motion `motion.div` with `initial={{opacity:0,y:18}} whileInView={{opacity:1,y:0}}`
    62→  for entrance animations.
    63→- Use shadcn `Button`, `Card`, `Input`, `Label`, `Tabs`, `Badge`, `Progress`, `Avatar`,
    64→  `Table`, `Dialog`, `Select`, `Switch`, `Slider`, `Checkbox`, `RadioGroup`, `Tooltip`,
    65→  `Separator`, `ScrollArea` from `@/components/ui/*`.
    66→- Use `lucide-react` icons.
    67→- Use `recharts` for any chart (line, bar, radar, pie). Dark theme colors:
    68→  `#a855f7` violet, `#06b6d4` cyan, `#10b981` emerald, `#f59e0b` amber, `#f43f5e` rose.
    69→
    70→## Footer rule
    71→Every screen ends naturally — no artificial "End of page" markers. Use `<footer>`
    72→with `border-t border-white/5 mt-16` for marketing screens. App screens (dashboard etc.)
    73→don't need a footer.
